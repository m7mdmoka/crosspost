CrossPost Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

CrossPost is a personal tool that runs only on its owner's own computer. There is no CrossPost server, and no data is sent to the developer.

What data CrossPost accesses

  • YouTube (Google): permission to upload videos to the channel you connect (youtube.upload) and to read that channel's name and picture (youtube.readonly).
  • TikTok and Instagram: permission to publish videos to the account you connect and to read its name and picture.

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload the videos you choose to the accounts you choose, and to show which accounts are connected. It is not used for advertising, sold, or shared with anyone.

Where the data is stored

Login tokens, account names and your post history are saved in a file on your own computer. Videos are kept only until the upload finishes, then deleted from CrossPost's folder.

Removing access

You can disconnect an account in CrossPost at any time, which deletes its saved login. You can also remove CrossPost's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

CrossPost's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for CrossPost.
