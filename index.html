CrossPost

CrossPost is a personal tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It lets the owner upload one video, with one title, to their own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts at the same time, and keep those accounts organized into separate profiles.

How it uses Google data

When you connect a YouTube channel, CrossPost asks Google for permission to upload videos to that channel and to read the channel's name and picture so it can show which channel is connected. It does not use your Google data for anything else.
